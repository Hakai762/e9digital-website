What's Possible

A few examples —
not the full list

These are real automations we can build, shown by industry to make them concrete. They're starting points, not a fixed menu. If a task in your business is repetitive and follows a pattern, it can almost certainly be automated — these just show the shape of what's possible.

Today

New client enquiry comes in

A staff member manually checks for conflicts of interest against existing clients, types up an intake form, drafts an engagement letter from scratch, and emails back and forth to book a consultation. It takes a day or two, and the client waits.

→
With E9 Digital

Intake runs itself

The enquiry triggers an automatic conflict check against your case management system, a tailored intake questionnaire is sent instantly, the engagement letter is drafted from your template, and the client books their own slot in your calendar.

2 days → 20 mins New client intake handled in minutes instead of days — with zero missed conflict checks and a faster, more professional first impression.
Today

Client sends a pile of invoices

A bookkeeper opens each PDF, reads off the supplier, amount, VAT and date, types it into Xero or QuickBooks line by line, and double-checks for errors. Multiply that across every client, every week — it's hours of repetitive data entry.

→
With E9 Digital

Invoices process themselves

Invoices arriving by email are read automatically — supplier, amount, VAT and date extracted and posted straight into your accounting software. Anything unusual gets flagged for a human to review, so accuracy actually goes up.

6–8 hrs/week saved per bookkeeper on manual data entry — time that goes back into advisory work and more clients.
Today

An enquiry lands at 8pm

Someone fills in the form about a listing on a Friday evening. Nobody sees it until Monday morning. By then they've already booked a viewing with the agency that replied first. The lead — and the commission — is gone.

→
With E9 Digital

You reply before they leave the page

The enquiry gets an instant, personalised reply with the property details, similar listings, and a link to book a viewing then and there. The lead is logged in your CRM and the agent is notified — all within seconds, day or night.

< 60 seconds first response, 24/7 — so you're the agency that wins the viewing instead of the one that replies too late.
Today

50 CVs land for one role

A consultant opens every CV one by one, reads through them, mentally matches each against the job spec, and tries to remember who looked promising. Strong candidates slip through simply because there wasn't time to get to them.

→
With E9 Digital

The shortlist builds itself

Every CV is read and scored against the role's real criteria the moment it arrives. The consultant opens their inbox to a ranked shortlist with the strongest matches at the top — and can spend their time actually talking to candidates.

50 CVs in minutes screened in the time it used to take to read five — faster placements and no good candidate left buried in the pile.
